> There is no restriction on linking to individual pages within the site. As with all web sites, there is the possibility of those pages being updated or moved.

> **12. Why can't I link directly to Messybeast photos?**
> Hot-linking my images to include them in your own pages or on forums uses up my bandwidth and costs me money. Messybeast is a not-for-profit site so I don't want to pay for other people to use my bandwidth. If you want to feature the images from here on a forum or bulletin board, you will have to copy that image to your own webspace and link to it there; that way you will be using your own bandwidth.

**What that means here:**
- **Keep the copyright and credit intact.** That's why this README exists.
- **Never hot-link messybeast images.** Use these local copies, which is exactly what Q12 asks for.
- **Ask first before adapting.** If the calculator ever shows an *adapted* version of one of these images or texts (e.g. a redrawn white-grade chart or eye chart in the UI), the "edited or adapted version — please ask first" clause applies. Email via the contents page before shipping it.
