Cat Coat Genetics Calculator ᨐ

Set the genotype at every coat locus — colour, pattern, white, fur and ears — and see the cat it makes: the breeder-style phenotype name, what it carries, and a live portrait.

    An educational toy, not a breeding tool: real cats add polygenic modifiers, rufousing, and a few loci science hasn’t cloned yet (Dilute-Modifier, Inhibitor, Spotted, Wide Band). A two-parent offspring calculator is the planned next step — the genetics engine here is already pure and Punnett-ready.

    The face on rex and hairless cats is hand-drawn by the repository owner (hairless.svg).
