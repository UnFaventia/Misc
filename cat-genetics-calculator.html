Set the genotype at every coat locus — colour, pattern, white, fur and ears — and see the cat it makes, or pair a queen and a tom and see every kitten they can produce, with exact odds and the genotypes behind each one.

Kittens

    Every gene is treated as segregating independently. Dilute-Modifier, Silver, Spotted and Wide Band are uncloned and can’t be DNA-tested, so odds that depend on them follow the single-gene model rather than a test result. How much white a spotted kitten shows is polygenic, so white spotting is named as the genotype’s range.

      Exact Mendelian odds for the genes that can be DNA-tested; model estimates for the rest. Real cats add polygenic modifiers, rufousing, and a few loci science hasn’t cloned yet (Dilute-Modifier, Inhibitor, Spotted, Wide Band) — treat anything that depends on those as a prediction, not a test result.
